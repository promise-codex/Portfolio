The story

About me.

My curiosity has always lived at the edges — the places where electrical engineering runs into medicine, automation, and intelligence. I recently finished my B.Sc. in Electronic & Electrical Engineering at Obafemi Awolowo University with a CGPA of 4.64, but the grades are only half the story. What I really care about is building systems that solve problems people actually have.

That's taken me in a lot of directions: co-leading a surgical robotics research team, interning on well-construction cementing at SLB (where I designed a system fusing RFID, computer vision, and pressure digitisation — now my final-year project), and working on analog IC and FPGA design at an engineering hub. Along the way I've co-authored four papers accepted at NIGERCON 2026, spanning surgical robotics, medical imaging, wearable rehabilitation, and energy policy.

The other half of me is people. As Secretary General of IEEE OAU and Director of Academics in my campus fellowship, I've organised flagship events, mentored over 1,500 students, and spoken everywhere from departmental panels to faculty-wide sessions. I like making rooms work, helping people grow, and — fair warning — I'm genuinely good company in a conversation.
